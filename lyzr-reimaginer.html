FAQ

Questions teams ask before the first workshop.

Get the security brief arrow_forward

What happens in the 72-hour prototype?

You walk us through one process. Our prototyping team builds the agents and shows them working on your scenario within 72 hours. You keep the readout, whatever you decide.

What data do you need for the prototype?

Synthetic or anonymised data is fine. Live systems only come in once you decide to build it for real.

Where does it run?

In your cloud, on hardware you own, or fully air-gapped. You can also run it on Lyzr’s cloud.

Who owns what gets built?

You do. The agents, the workflows and what they learn stay in your environment and belong to you.

How is it governed?

Every agent ships with evals, guardrails and a full audit trail, and can be governed alongside your other agents in Opencontroller.

Which certifications does Lyzr hold?

SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001 and ISO 42001, with practices aligned to HIPAA and GDPR. Reports are in the Lyzr trust center.

Can our team run it after you leave?

Yes. Everything is built on Lyzr Agent Studio in your environment, and your team is trained to run and change it.

What does it cost after the test drive?

If you go ahead, you take the Reimaginer engineering package for the year and pay for agents in production. Drafts and tests cost nothing.
