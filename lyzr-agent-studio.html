FAQ

Get answers before your first build.

What is Lyzr Agent Studio?

The platform your developers use to build, test, deploy and govern AI agents and multi-step workflows.

Who uses it?

Developers and platform teams build in Studio. Business teams can start in Architect, which hands agents over to Studio.

Which models and clouds does it support?

Any major model provider or open model, deployed on Lyzr cloud, AWS, Google Cloud, Azure, your VPC or on-prem.

Can it run inside our network?

Yes. The full studio can run in your VPC, on-prem or air-gapped.

How do we test agents before they go live?

Simulated users, red-team attacks and evals run before release, and failures from live traffic become new tests.

Can a person approve steps in a workflow?

Yes. Any step can wait for a named person before the workflow continues.

Is Studio certified?

Lyzr holds SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001 and ISO 42001.

Can we build products for our own customers on it?

Yes. You can build agents into your own product and offer them under your own brand.

Show us the agent you want in production.

We’ll walk you through Studio on a use case of yours.
