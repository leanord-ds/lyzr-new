FAQ

Questions about Lyzr Agent Studio.

What is Lyzr Agent Studio?

Lyzr Agent Studio is the platform to build, orchestrate, test, deploy, observe and govern AI agents. Teams build agents, apps and voice agents, connect knowledge bases and tools, chain them into Superflows and ship them to any cloud, a private VPC or on-prem hardware.

What types of agents can I build with Lyzr Agent Studio?

Single agents for a focused task, managerial agents that coordinate other agents, multi-step Superflows that chain agents, tools and human approvals, and voice agents for calls and IVR. Teams build workbenches for finance, HR, sales, marketing, IT ops, customer support and procurement.

Can I customize agents to my business workflows?

Yes. You choose the model, instructions, knowledge, tools, skills and global contexts for every agent, and Superflows let you decide each step, each model and exactly where a person approves before anything moves.

Can I run the agents locally?

Yes. You can run Agent Studio and its agents locally or inside your private infrastructure, for full control over data, models and logs.

Can I deploy Agent Studio in my own VPC?

Yes. Agent Studio installs in your cloud account on AWS, Google Cloud or Azure, inside a private VPC with no public endpoints. Agents, knowledge bases, traces and credentials stay in your network.

Can I run Agent Studio on-prem or fully air-gapped?

Yes. Agent Studio runs on servers in your own data center, and fully air-gapped on Lyzr Optimus, our on-prem appliance, with open-weight models running locally and no outside connection.

Does any of our data leave our environment?

Not when you deploy in your VPC or on-prem. Data only goes where you allow it, for example to a hosted model provider you choose. Run open-weight models locally and nothing needs to leave your network.

Which models and clouds does Agent Studio work with?

Agent Studio is model- and cloud-agnostic. Connect Amazon Bedrock, Google Vertex AI, OpenAI, Anthropic, Azure OpenAI, Google Gemini, Mistral AI, Meta, xAI, Groq, Nvidia and Hugging Face, set fallbacks, and let auto-model routing pick the right model for each request.

Does Agent Studio adhere to data privacy regulations?

Yes. Lyzr is SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001 and ISO 42001 certified, and HIPAA, GDPR and CCPA compliant. Data is encrypted, and in VPC or on-prem deployments it is processed inside your own infrastructure.

Can I white-label Lyzr Agent Blocks?

Yes. Take Agent Blocks, embed them in your platform or your own agent builder, put your brand on them and sell them to your customers.

How much does Agent Studio cost?

Agent Studio is free to get started. As your team and usage grow, it runs as a usage-based SaaS plan on Lyzr’s cloud. Deployments in your own VPC or on-prem are custom-priced for your environment.

What support is available?

Our applied AI team can build your first agents and workflows for you. After onboarding, our 50+ implementation partners take on delivery and ongoing support, and self-serve teams have the docs and Lyzr University.
