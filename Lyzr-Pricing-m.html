The agent-month

One governed agent, running in production, for one month.

The same object as an FTE-month in a labor plan. A governed agent has its own identity, its own policy and its own deployment lifecycle. Runs, sessions, replicas, versions and clones are not separate agents, just as a person working overtime is not a second person.

Interns are free

You do not put someone on the payroll on the day you interview them. Agents in development, in Architect, in Studio or in a sandbox pointed at UAT draw nothing. You pay when an agent is promoted into production with an identity, a policy and a lifecycle.

One workflow is one agent

A SuperFlow workflow with twenty nodes draws once a month, at the class of its most privileged node. A shared service used by six workflows is billed once for the estate. Good architecture is the cheapest architecture.

Activity moves the rate

An agent that completes fewer than 100 units of work in a month draws a quarter of its class rate. One that completes more than 100,000 draws one class up. Class C is always full rate.

Questions

Questions finance teams ask.

What counts as one agent?
Anything with its own identity, its own policy and its own deployment lifecycle. The test: can it be promoted, permissioned and rolled back without touching anything else? Versions, clones, replicas and specialists inside a workflow are part of their parent.
Do you charge for tokens?
No. You pay your model provider for tokens, or run open-weight models on your own hardware. Lyzr never adds a second line on the same unit of work.
How do you tell development from production?
By the runtime path, not a checkbox. An agent bound to your production identity provider, production tools and production data plane is in production. Everything else is an intern and draws nothing.
Who decides an agent's class?
The controller does, from the permissions actually granted. A read-only tool keeps an agent at A. A write into an enterprise system makes it B. An attached sandbox makes it C.
What happens when we outgrow a block?
Additional agent-months bill monthly in arrears at $1,700 each, or you buy the next block of 150 for $250K. Unused agent-months roll over on multi-year terms.
Do agents we did not build on Lyzr cost agent-months?
No. Agents from Copilot, Bedrock, LangGraph or anywhere else are governed under Opencontroller at the flat price. Agent-months are only for agents built and run on Lyzr.
What if a production agent only runs a few times a month?
It is part-time and draws a quarter of its class rate for that month: 0.25 for Class B, 0.06 for Class A. Class C stays at full rate because a sandbox with production credentials is standing capacity.
What counts as a run?
One unit of work completed: a request answered, a ticket closed, a claim decided, a record processed. A batch that processes 10,000 records is 10,000 runs.
