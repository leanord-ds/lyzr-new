FAQ

Get answers before your first call.

What is Openfinetuner?

Lyzr’s fine-tuning platform. It trains small open models on your own data for your repetitive work, and routes each request to the most cost-effective model that handles it well.

Will quality drop?

No request moves until your own model matches the frontier model on your own tests. Everything else stays where it is.

Do our agents have to change?

No. They keep calling the same endpoint, and the switch happens behind it.

Which models can we fine-tune?

Open-weight models such as Llama, Qwen, Mistral, Gemma, Phi and DeepSeek.

Where does our data go?

Nowhere you don’t choose. Training data, weights and prompts stay in your cloud or on your own hardware.

What hardware do we need?

GPUs you already have, a GPU cloud account, or a Lyzr Optimus device.

What stays on frontier models?

Advanced coding, long multi-step reasoning, and anything where a smaller model hasn’t proven itself.

How do we know what we’ll save?

We look at your current agent traffic with you and show which requests could move, before anything changes.
