Our method

RADAR. One method, from problem to outcome realized.

Five stages, run in order, each closed by a gate, with a measured result at the end. Most enterprise AI work stops one stage too early: a deployed agent is not yet adopted, and an adopted agent has not yet paid for itself.

Redesign the process before you automate it.

  • What happens

    We sit with the people doing the work and map every step, handoff, system and delay. Then we design where agents act and where people approve, and capture today's numbers as the baseline.

  • Led by

    Reimaginer consultant

  • Gate

    Your business owner agrees the problem is worth solving and signs off the baseline.

Agree on what winning means, then prove it.

  • What happens

    The design becomes a business case with outcome targets, walked through IT, security and risk. Then we prove it on your own work with the 72-hour prototype or the four-week pilot.

  • Led by

    Business case lead and the prototype team

  • Gate

    The scorecard is signed and you decide 1.

Build on your real systems and ship to production.

  • What happens

    Forward deployed engineers build the agents on your infrastructure and connect them to the systems the work already runs on. Evals on real cases, guardrails, audit trail, user acceptance testing and IT approval.

  • Led by

    Forward deployed engineers and a platform engineer

  • Gate

    Live in production and passing the evaluation thresholds in the scorecard.

Make the new way the only way.

  • What happens

    We train each team in its own work, name champions, fix wherever people work around the agents, and retire the old steps so the old way stops being an option. Agents sit in the tools people already use.

  • Led by

    Customer success, with the Reimaginer consultant

  • Gate

    Adoption target met and the old process retired.

Measure it against the baseline. Then the next one.

  • What happens

    Results are measured against the baseline with your business owner and finance: cycle time, cost per transaction, error rate and hours returned to people.

  • Led by

    Expansion lead, with your business owner and finance

  • Gate

    Outcome report signed and the next problem named.

The product

What a reimagined process looks like when it runs.

Every engagement ends in a running workflow, not a slide deck. This is vendor invoice reconciliation in Lyzr SuperFlow: data pulls, matching, checks, an LLM and a human approval, in one flow your IT team can read end to end.

  1. 1TriggerStarts on its own every morning.
  2. 2GatherPulls the ERP bundle, bank statement and vendor invoices in parallel.
  3. 3MatchBuilds one reconciliation dataset, runs a three-way match and classifies every line.
  4. 4CheckFlags suspected fraud, stops the run and raises an alert.
  5. 5SummarizeOtherwise an LLM writes the payment batch summary.
  6. 6ApproveA person signs off before anything is paid.

Durable

A run that waits days for an approval picks up exactly where it stopped, and nothing is paid twice.

Traced

Every step, tool call and token is recorded, so audit can follow any run step by step.

Reviewable

IT reads the whole process on one canvas and signs off before it goes live.

Revenue assurance, CFO office

Revenue leakage, found by checking contracts against drone imagery.

  • 2 weeksfrom kickoff to a working multi-agent system
  • 90%+match accuracy between order forms and installed equipment
  • Day 1agents at work, with no semantic data model to build first
  • Before

    Crown Castle, critical infrastructure for the United States, could not reliably match the order forms signed with carriers against the receivers actually installed on its towers. Every mismatch leaked revenue, and finding them took heavy manual effort.

  • Reimagined

    Rather than spending a year on a semantic data model first, Reimaginers had agents working from day one. Image agents read drone imagery of each tower, reconciliation agents compare it with the order form, and variances go to people to resolve.

Now

A two-week pilot passed 90% accuracy and became a signed deal. The same revenue leakage pattern now runs for other Lyzr customers.
