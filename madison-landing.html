FAQ

Questions we get asked.

How is Madison different from a GRC tool?

A GRC tool holds the record. Madison does the work behind it: finding the rules that apply, mapping each change to your policies and controls, checking controls for proof and linking evidence as it's produced. A named owner signs every step.

We already have a GRC platform. Do we replace it?

No. It stays your system of record. Madison sits on top of it, does the work, and feeds the results back in.

Does Madison decide whether we are compliant?

No. Agents propose; a named owner at your bank decides and signs.

Where does Madison run?

Your choice: in your own cloud, or on Lyzr's secure cloud. Either way it reads your core systems and never writes to them.

Who at the bank uses it?

Compliance, control testing, internal audit and risk, from one record.

How is our data protected?

SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001 and ISO 42001. Reports at security.lyzr.ai.
