    Who is the ISV program for?

    Software companies that want to add AI capabilities to their products, create branded agent offerings or govern existing agents. We work with partnerships, product and engineering teams to define the opportunity together.

    Can we offer this entirely under our brand?

    Yes. Private-labelling is part of the program. We work with you on the product surfaces, integration and customer experience. You retain your brand and customer relationship.

    Do we have to replace our existing AI stack?

    You can start with the capabilities your product needs. AgentBlocks supports modular integration, while OpenController can govern existing agents across frameworks and environments. We agree on the architecture with your engineering team.

    Can we deploy inside a customer’s environment?

    Yes. The partnership can include deployments in your cloud offering or inside customers’ cloud environments. For sovereign or air-gapped requirements, we scope the infrastructure, model and integration choices with your technical team.

    How does Lyzr support delivery and sales?

    The program includes dedicated engineering support, joint go-to-market planning and co-selling. We define the team, responsibilities and support scope around your product and customer requirements.
