FAQ

Get straight answers on sovereign AI.

What does sovereign AI mean for us?

Your agents, models and data run on infrastructure you control. No outside model vendor, cloud or internet connection is needed unless you choose one.

Do we need our own GPUs?

No. Use GPUs you already have, or a Lyzr Optimus device that arrives with everything installed.

Which models will we run?

Open-weight models such as Llama, Gemma, Mistral and Qwen, trained on your own data. Frontier models stay available wherever your policy allows.

Can it run with no internet connection at all?

Yes. Every part of the stack runs inside your perimeter, so it works fully air-gapped.

Who sets it up?

Our forward-deployed engineers, working with your team until it runs on its own.

Can we start with one department?

Yes. Most teams start with one department or one workflow, then grow from there.

How are agents governed?

Every agent is registered, owned and checked against your rules through Opencontroller, inside the same perimeter.

What happens if we stop working with Lyzr?

You keep your models, data and agents. Everything is built on open standards, so it all stays usable.
